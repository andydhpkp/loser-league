Loser League Help

How to play

The objective

Choose an NFL Team you expect to lose each week. A User can own more than one Track; every Track has its own Picks and elimination state.

Making Picks

Submit the final Pick for every active Track before the displayed deadline. A Track cannot choose the same NFL Team twice in one League Season. If a Pick is missing at the deadline, Loser League assigns an automatic Pick when the rules allow it.

Pick visibility

Current league Picks stay hidden until the league visibility rules allow them to be shown.

Wrong Picks and elimination

Your goal is to pick a losing Team. If that Team wins or ties, the Pick is a Wrong Pick and that Track is eliminated.

Week 1 buyback

After Week 1, eligible eliminated Tracks may receive a one-time Week 2 buyback offer. Make the decision before the Week 2 deadline. A pending request blocks Week 2 Picks until an admin resolves it or the deadline expires it.
